## 2. Strategic Link Building Pillars

### Pillar 1: High-Authority NZ Local Directories & Citations
* **Objective:** Establish foundational geo-relevance and NAP consistency across New Zealand business indexes.
* **Target Channels:** Yellow Pages NZ, Finda NZ, Localist, NZ Business Directory, Hotfrog NZ, Cylex NZ.
* **Target Landing Pages:** Homepage (`/`), Auckland (`/service-area/auckland`), Hamilton (`/service-area/hamilton`), Wellington (`/service-area/wellington`).

### Pillar 2: Industry & Security Association Mentions
* **Objective:** Capture high-trust industry topical authority.
* **Target Opportunities:** New Zealand Security Association (NZSA) member directory, Ministry of Justice licensing register citations, regional Chambers of Commerce (Auckland, Waikato, Wellington).

### Pillar 3: Commercial Client & Partner Co-Marketing
* **Objective:** Secure natural, high-relevance contextual backlinks from commercial partners.
* **Strategy:** Request featured logo mentions and case study link-backs on websites of commercial property developers, civil construction contractors, and property management firms serviced by Atlas Security.

### Pillar 4: Informational Content & PR Resource Outreach
* **Objective:** Earn high-authority editorial links by providing expert security advice to NZ commercial publications.
* **Target Assets:** High-value blog guides (`/blog/commercial-building-after-hours-security-checklist`, `/blog/static-guards-vs-mobile-patrols-nz`).
* **Target Outlets:** NZ Business Magazine, Property Council New Zealand resources, regional commercial property blogs.
